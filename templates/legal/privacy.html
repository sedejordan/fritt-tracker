Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data to make the Service work:

  • Email address - For login and notifications
  • Document titles and expiry dates - Your tracking data
  • Password hash - Encrypted, never stored as plain text
  • IP addresses - For rate limiting and security

We never sell your data to third parties or use it for marketing.

How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you when you log in
  • To store and display your documents
  • To send you important service updates (e.g., password resets)
  • To prevent abuse of the Service (rate limiting)

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database on Render.com. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all your documents at any time from the Delete Account page. When you delete, we permanently remove all your data from our database.

Cookies

We use cookies only for authentication and security:

  • Session cookie - Keeps you logged in
  • CSRF token - Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks

We don't use tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to run Fritt Tracker:

  • Render.com - Hosting and PostgreSQL database
  • Resend.com - Sending emails (password resets)
  • Sentry - Error monitoring (optional)

These services have their own privacy policies, but they only receive the data necessary to provide their service (e.g., your email for Resend, error logs for Sentry).

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request corrections to your data
  • Delete your account and data at any time
  • Opt out of communications (except essential service updates)

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Contact us at privacy@fritt.org
