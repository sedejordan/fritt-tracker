How to Track Employee Document Expiry: Right to Work, Licences and Certifications

By the Fritt Tracker team

When an employee's right to work, professional licence or safety certificate expires and nobody notices, the business carries the risk. It can mean fines, a worker who has to stop work, or a failed audit. The frustrating part is that these dates are known months or years ahead. What is usually missing is a system that surfaces them at the right time.

This guide covers which employee documents to track, what the rules look like in the UK, the US and Nigeria, and a simple process you can start this week.

Which employee documents expire

  • Right to work or work authorisation documents, for staff whose permission has an end date
  • Professional licences and registrations
  • Safety and training certificates, such as first aid, forklift or working at height
  • Driving licences and driver medicals for anyone who drives for work
  • Fixed term contract end dates and probation review dates
  • Any certification a client requires, such as site passes

UK: right to work follow up checks

UK employers must check that every new hire has the right to work before they start. If a worker's permission is time limited, you must carry out a follow up check before it expires to keep your statutory excuse against a civil penalty. The maximum penalty is up to £45,000 per illegal worker for a first breach and £60,000 for repeat breaches. You must keep the evidence for the length of employment plus two years.

The rules are also due to widen from 1 October 2026 to cover more working arrangements beyond conventional employment, so it is a good moment to review who you check and how you record it. Check the current Home Office guidance on GOV.UK.

US: reverifying work authorization

US employers complete Form I-9 for every new hire. When an employee's work authorization has an expiry date, the employer must reverify it on or before that date. Citizens and lawful permanent residents generally do not need reverification. Put every expiry date on a list and check the current guidance from US Citizenship and Immigration Services.

Nigeria: permits and professional licences

Foreign staff need valid residence and work documents for as long as they are employed, so record each permit's expiry date. Many professional bodies also require periodic renewal of practising licences for engineers, accountants, nurses and others. Check the rules of the body that regulates each role.

Set up a tracking process in five steps

  1. List the roles in your business and the documents each one needs.
  2. Record each employee's documents with the expiry date and the manager responsible.
  3. Set reminder windows based on how long each renewal takes.
  4. When a reminder arrives, act: request the new document, book the course or schedule the check.
  5. Update the record with the new expiry date as soon as the renewal is confirmed.

Handle employee data with care

Expiry tracking involves personal information, so keep what you store to a minimum. Record the document type, the expiry date and who is responsible. You rarely need to hold a full copy of an ID number in a tracker. Rules such as UK GDPR and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 apply to employee data, so limit access to the people who need it.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I remind employees to renew documents?

Match the reminder to how long the renewal takes. Ninety days works for right to work and licence renewals, and sixty days is often enough for safety certificates and driver medicals. Allow longer if a course or application has a waiting list.

Do I need a follow up right to work check for British citizens?

Generally no. British and Irish citizens have an ongoing right to work, so follow up checks apply to workers whose permission has an expiry date. Check current Home Office guidance for your situation.

Who should be responsible for tracking employee document expiry?

Name one accountable person, often an HR lead or operations manager, and one backup. Individual managers can renew their own team's documents, but someone should oversee the full list.
