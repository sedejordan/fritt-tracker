Insurance Policy & COI Expiry Tracking

A lapsed policy can cost you a contract, a claim, or your business. Fritt Tracker keeps every insurance policy and certificate of insurance on one dashboard with reminders before anything expires.

What you can track

  • General liability policies
  • Professional liability / E&O
  • Workers' compensation
  • Commercial auto and property
  • Cyber liability and D&O
  • Vendor certificates of insurance (COIs)

How it works

  1. Add each policy or COI with its expiration date and renewal contact.
  2. Fritt Tracker shows you what's expiring soon, at a glance.
  3. You get email reminders at 90, 60, and 30 days before expiration.
  4. Renew with time to shop the market if the renewal quote is bad.
