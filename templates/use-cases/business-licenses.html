Business License & Permit Renewal Tracking

Every license, permit, and inspection has an expiration date. Miss one and you're looking at fines, forced closures, or lost contracts. Fritt Tracker keeps them all in one place and reminds you before it's too late.

What you can track

  • Business operating licenses
  • Sales tax / VAT registrations
  • Health & safety inspections
  • Professional certifications
  • Insurance policies
  • Vehicle registrations and inspections

How it works

  1. Add each license with its expiration date.
  2. Fritt Tracker color-codes it: 🟦 Safe, 🟢 Good, 🟠 Warning, 🔴 Urgent, ⚫ Expired.
  3. You get email reminders at 120, 60, 30, and 7 days before expiration.
  4. Renew the license, then update the date in one click.
