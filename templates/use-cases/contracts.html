Contract & Agreement Renewal Tracking

Know when every client contract, NDA, vendor agreement, and SaaS subscription renews — and when the cancellation window closes. No more surprises, no more auto-renewal traps.

What you can track

  • Client contracts and retainers
  • Vendor and supplier agreements
  • NDAs and consulting agreements
  • SaaS subscriptions and software licenses
  • Commercial leases and property agreements
  • Service level agreements (SLAs)

How it works

  1. Add each contract with its cancellation deadline — not just the end date.
  2. Fritt Tracker colour-codes it by urgency.
  3. You get reminders at 90 days, 30 days, and 7 days before the cancellation window closes.
  4. Renew, renegotiate, or cancel — with time to decide.
