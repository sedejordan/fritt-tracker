Passport & Visa Expiration Tracking

Most countries require 6 months of passport validity beyond your return date. Fritt Tracker reminds you 9 months, 6 months, and 30 days before your passport — or any visa — expires, so you never get turned away at the airport.

What you can track

  • Passports for every family member
  • Visas (tourist, work, student, residence)
  • Driver's licenses and international driving permits
  • Travel insurance policies
  • Loyalty programme tier expirations
  • Global Entry / TSA PreCheck / trusted traveller programmes

How it works

  1. Add each passport, visa, or travel document with its expiration date.
  2. Fritt Tracker shows the status at a glance: 🟦 Safe, 🟢 Good, 🟠 Warning, 🔴 Urgent, ⚫ Expired.
  3. You get email reminders at 9 months, 6 months, and 30 days before expiration.
  4. Once renewed, update the date with one click.
